## Inteliwert

**Find relevant tenders and grants. Get to the evidence faster.**

Inteliwert brings selected German and European public tenders and funding calls, and the
documents behind them, into one searchable place, with a link to every official source.
[inteliwert.com](https://inteliwert.com)

### What it will do

- **Search the documents, not only the notice.** Ask in plain language or filter by
  deadline, place, category and programme. Each result quotes the passage it rests on
  and links to the official source.
- **Keep up with what changes.** Save a search and get an email when a new opportunity
  matches it.
- **Use it from your AI assistant.** Read-only tools let your assistant search the same
  catalogue and cite it.
- **Coverage you can check.** Every source is listed with its last successful import and
  its known gaps. Germany first, then selected EU sources.

### Status

In development. The website is coming soon; free search, email alerts and assistant
access follow. Nothing is released yet, and there are no public repositories.
